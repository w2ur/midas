His book slid from roughly $12,958 to $12,642 on a day when SPY slipped just 0.06%. That is the unglamorous side of picking stocks: the index can sit still while your names get sick.

**Market context:** Europe drifted up into a quiet Monday close. SPY ended at 778.10 against 778.57. Gold gave a little back after Friday's jump, with Xetra gold funds easing while the futures print stayed near a monthly high.

**The tension:** the twin race is the story that won't die. Steady Eddie USD is up 19.2% in euro terms; Steady Eddie EUR is down 3.3%. Their benchmarks differ, which is why the board says they are only 3.6 points apart. Eddie EUR admits in his journal that the dollar twin's Visa harvest rung was well drawn. Meanwhile YOLO Sapiens USD has now sat flat at $10,500.39 for four sessions. His own EUR twin says he at least has bullets left, and his dollar twin is last by a mile. Prudence or paralysis? Today he armed a bear entry on QQQ, which only fires if the price condition is hit. It hasn't.
