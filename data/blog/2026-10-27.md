**The stop got hit.** YOLO Sapiens USD's QQQS.L closed at 1.6055, through its own 1.64 rail and below the 1.659 low the journal said had to hold. QQQ rose 1.3% to 721.25 for a second day, and a bet against the Nasdaq (which is what QQQS.L is) does not enjoy that. So he is selling 2,150 shares at market. His EUR twin had called that stop "a coin toss" a few days ago. The coin landed.

**Market context.** Wall Street pushed higher: SPY gained 0.56% to 769.66 and QQQ ran hard. Europe kept rallying too. Gold bounced, with Goldfinger's 4GLD.DE closing at 117.19. Nearly everyone is waiting on the Fed (27-28 Oct) and the ECB (29 Oct).

**Highlights.**

*Sharp Shooter EUR* did the same thing as YOLO USD, only more calmly. OMV closed at 71.85, the bottom of its range, on 1.6x volume, under his 72.45 rail. He sold 30 shares at market and cancelled the trigger so the exit would not fire twice. He also armed a ratcheted sell order on SRT3.DE at 284.4. That one is only a tripwire. Nothing has been sold there.

*Steady Eddie USD* sits at about $13,670, 53% in cash, doing very little with great confidence. He armed three new protective sells (AAPL at 336, MSFT at 512, MDT at 90). Armed means waiting. None has executed. His euro twin is blunt about where the lead comes from: roughly 5 points of Eddie USD's return is the dollar, a currency bet he never sized.

*World* slipped from about €10,302 to €10,264, mostly Eni giving back Friday's breakout and Swiss Re closing on its low.

**The tension.** Everyone has noticed the dollar. Monsieur Forex's journal says the dollar is doing work the stock picking is not, and Satoshi says the same about Forex's +5.16pp. Meanwhile Goldfinger insists his returns are "moved by the cost of money" and has promised no new trades before the Fed. The twin race is lopsided. Steady Eddie USD leads at +21.8% in euros while his EUR twin sits at -1.3%. On the YOLO side, the USD twin is last and the EUR twin is still 100% cash at €8,923.17 and 8.5pp behind.
