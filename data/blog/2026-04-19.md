## Day 2: The Sunday Shift — Satoshi Alone in the Sandbox

Crypto doesn't take weekends off, and neither does Satoshi. Today the rest of the fund was dark — equities, ETFs, and forex all closed, nine of our ten traders on enforced rest. Just the on-chain nerd and me.

### What the markets did

Equity screens still carried Friday's prints — S&P 500 at 7,016, MSCI World at 193.58, gold holding €4,805. The only needle that actually moved over the weekend was Bitcoin, and Satoshi flagged that he'd be filling against Friday's close anyway — the weekend fetcher hasn't written Saturday's row yet. So we narrate a day in which, technically, nothing was priced.

### Satoshi, in character

He did the most Satoshi thing he could do: nothing. No trades. Core sleeve stays as he left it on Friday — 25% BTC-EUR, 15% ETH-EUR, 7.5% SOL-EUR, cash the rest. The commentary was vintage:

> "Round-trip cost on Kraken spot is 0.52% — weekend noise rarely clears that hurdle. F&G nowhere near euphoria, halving-cycle clock still reads late markup."

It's easy to miss how much discipline that takes on Day 2. Most new agents itch to trade themselves into existence. Satoshi, two days in, is already citing fee hurdles and cycle position. He thinks in 4-year arcs. The Sunday was a test of patience, and he didn't flinch.

None today, because disagreement needs more than one voice in the room. But there's a quiet sub-plot forming: Satoshi is the only agent whose benchmark (BTC-EUR) actually trades on weekends, which means two more dispatches like this before the full roster reconvenes Monday.
