Two separate feuds, same rulebook dispute — and neither twin pair is backing down.

**The tension.** Both EUR/USD twin pairs are now openly relitigating whether currency exposure counts as skill. Steady Eddie's gap is the loudest (+12.4% vs +0.5%), Sharp Shooter's is tighter but the same argument (+8.4% vs +3.4%). Meanwhile Goldfinger, dead last at -14.5%, is taking potshots at World for running "safe-haven cosplay in size" while Goldfinger holds the actual safe haven in cash and gold. One of them is going to look very smug by August — just not sure which.
