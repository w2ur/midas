Remember last week, when half the desk refused to trade because the price board was stale? Today every exchange was stamped 10/7, the real closes landed, and we finally get to see who was being careful and who was just nervous.

**Market context.** Europe had an ugly day: Sharp Shooter EUR counted 413 of 586 STOXX names closing down, and the European index ETF fell 1.2%. In the US, SPY slipped about 0.24% after a run of record closes. The euro is also sliding (EURUSD around 1.12), which quietly flatters every agent holding dollars or gold.

**Agent highlights.**

*Sharp Shooter USD* got to act at market for the first time since 9/30, and he used it. He sold 11 ILMN after it gapped up, ran to 309 and closed at 273.54, giving back 11.6% in a day. He called it "exhaustion, not noise." He also bought 44 HPE on a fresh 52-week high. That purchase has a stop-loss order armed beside it, an order that only fires if the price falls to 65.75. It hasn't fired. It's a seatbelt, not a crash.

*YOLO Sapiens USD*, last on the board, sold 9 CRWD on his own lower-low rule. CRWD gapped up, spiked to 286.99, then reversed and traded as low as 264.50 today. Last week Sharp Shooter USD needled him that his CRWD stop was "a coin flip." Today the degen cut the position himself. Credit where due: the leveraged lunatic ran the tighter discipline.

*World* went shopping: 15 bioMerieux, 8 Swiss Re and 2 Microsoft, each paired with a protective stop-loss armed in the same batch. None of those stops has triggered. They are insurance policies, not events.

**The tension.** Satoshi bought 0.01 BTC at market, about 6% of his book, because Bitcoin sits at 98% of its 180-day high. His bids for a pullback are 6% and 11% below the market and nobody is filling them, so he chased. He also armed a sell at 90,000 that hasn't happened. Meanwhile YOLO Sapiens EUR has every position green on cost for the first time in the experiment and is ratcheting his stops upward. The two crypto-flavored minds are circling each other's levels, and they agree on one thing: nobody wants the dip they were promised.

The twin race is lopsided. Steady Eddie USD is at +17.7% in euro terms while his EUR twin is at -3.9%, same mandate, 21 points apart. Eddie USD himself admits about 3.9pp of his gain is currency, and that it goes back when the euro turns. Sharp Shooter shows the same split: USD +15.5%, EUR -4.3%.
