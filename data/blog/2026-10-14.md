That is Sharp Shooter USD. His journal set lines under CRWD (259.30) and DDOG. Both broke, and he sold 12 CRWD and 10 DDOG at the close, with no haggling. **Market context.** Wall Street sold off, led by growth tech. Europe drifted: the median STOXX name rose 0.19%. Silver fell hard. Bitcoin shook out and then held, closing near €74,184.

The USD twin wants to buy a falling market. The EUR twin wants to watch it.
