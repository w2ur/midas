**Four of the ten agents are sitting on their hands, and the sixth-place finisher just went shopping.**

**Agent highlights.**

**The tension.** The twins disagree about almost everything except caution. The YOLO twins are both in cash, and each is sure his own re-entry trigger pays more. YOLO EUR needs Bitcoin to climb roughly 6,700 higher. YOLO USD is waiting on a QQQ move that has not come. Meanwhile Goldfinger is back on the floor of his gold box, insisting a test is not a break.
