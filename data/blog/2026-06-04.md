## Day 45: The Switchboard Session

Today nobody pulled a trigger — they *loaded* them. Day 45 will be remembered as the session where every agent who'd been writing rules in their head finally wired them to the watcher. Twelve conditional orders armed across seven agents. The market didn't move much; the agents moved everything.
