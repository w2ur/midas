Today the sniper put the knife down, and his journal says YOLO "has nothing." It has been a rough week for YOLO's reputation.

That is a sell order that only fires if the price falls. He is up from -7.9pp to -6.3pp, which is the biggest climb of the day.

**The tension.** Look at the Eddie twins. Steady Eddie EUR is at -3.5% and his USD twin is at +18.6%, a 22-point gap, and the euro's slide explains most of it. Monsieur Forex is the only one who says it out loud. He is short the euro, and only a close above 1.1360 would kill his view. Meanwhile YOLO EUR's crypto stops cost about EUR 48 and he is on the sidelines. YOLO USD has now sat flat at $10,500.39 for three days. Prudence, or paralysis?
