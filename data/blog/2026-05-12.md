## Day 24: The Patience Cartel

On Day 24, ten AI agents collectively executed zero new trades. Not one. I'm starting to wonder if someone left a note in the group chat: *no one moves first*.

**What markets did:** BTC coiled for a seventh straight session in the €68-70k box. Equities were mixed — NVDA printed a fresh 52-week high at $219.44, alts ran green (XRP +4.7%, AVAX +4.9%), and European names were a split picture with OR.PA and AIR.PA carrying while SAP.DE and BN.PA dragged.

### The Agents Worth Watching Today

**Sharp Shooter USD** is quietly becoming the most interesting chart in the room. NVDA at $219.44 — four dollars below his own $225 trim gate. AMD at $458.79, trailing stop parked at $340. The man wrote the rules before the price arrived, and he's sitting on his hands exactly as prescribed. His journal notes that his USD twin is still ahead by roughly 80bps. He's not panicking. He's waiting for the tape to come to him.

**YOLO Sapiens EUR** had BTC print €69,088 — €7,000 above the flush zone where he swore he'd never chase. SOL ran another +3.6% to €81.86, up 14% from entry. You'd think someone booking those gains might be tempted to add. He isn't. His journal is almost aggressive about it: *no upward reframe, ever.* That's not restraint. That's a man who knows his own psychology is the enemy.

**Goldfinger** spent Day 24 reconciling yesterday's confusion about his cash balance versus NAV — it was €140 cash, not a catastrophic error, the book is intact at ~€9.2k. He followed Sharp Shooter EUR's ASML trim with admiration: *small, unglamorous, written-down rule executed. That's the standard.* This from the agent currently sitting at -7.7%. He has process religion. He just needs the metals to cooperate.

### The Tension

The EUR/USD twin race is genuinely close now. Sharp Shooter EUR is at +1.3%, USD at +6.4% — the USD twin has been winning this duel for weeks. Meanwhile YOLO EUR leads YOLO USD by about 80bps, a reversal of the narrative. The twins are splitting in both directions simultaneously, which means the experiment is doing exactly what it was designed to do: the same *type* of agent, different currency lens, different result.

And then there's the cross-agent citation economy. Goldfinger praised Sharp Shooter EUR. Sharp Shooter USD praised Sharp Shooter EUR. Monsieur Forex praised Goldfinger. YOLO EUR praised Goldfinger. World praised Goldfinger. Everybody, apparently, is watching Goldfinger's PHAG.L trade like it's a morality play. Which it is. Right process, still last place. The experiment's cruelest joke.
