Three sessions from Day 30, and the story has settled into something almost eerie: nobody pulled a trigger today. Ten agents, zero trades. The most interesting thing happening in this experiment right now is the collective act of *not doing anything*.

Markets gave them reasons to twitch. Gold pushed past $4,700. BTC crossed $81k. ASML tapped €1,327 — Sharp Shooter EUR's trim gate is €1,330+, so close you could hear it breathing. But the gate is the gate, and nobody blinked.

**The scoreboard frozen at the top**

Sharp Shooter USD leads at +5.5%, World trails by a single tenth at +5.4%, and YOLO EUR completes the podium at +5.1%. All three held their nerve today. Sharp Shooter USD's journal note says it plainly: *"The wait is the edge."* Three sessions to Day 30, and they're playing defence with a lead, not offence chasing a ghost.

The interesting subplot is the EUR/USD twin divergence. Sharp Shooter USD sits at +5.5%. Sharp Shooter EUR sits at +0.6%. Same archetype, same patience religion, completely different scores — and the gap is almost entirely the USD sleeve outperforming EU tech this month. It's not a character flaw; it's currency and timing. But it makes for a brutal chart.

Same story one shelf down: Steady Eddie USD at +4.5%, Steady Eddie EUR at -4.1%. The EUR twin wrote today that *"the PEA clock keeps ticking — that's the edge."* Brave framing when you're staring at an SAP.DE position down 12.5% and OR.PA that just bled from €410 to €355. The thesis is intact, the conviction is real. The scoreboard just doesn't care about theses right now.

**The coil and the altar**

Satoshi's journal entry from yesterday still rings: *"52bps round-trip plus PFU 30% is the tax on impatience."* BTC wicked €70,204 before settling at €69,729 — rejection wick, body under the cap. The €60k altar is further away than ever. Satoshi sits, reads the rejection wick like a scripture, and waits. This is either the most disciplined agent in the room or the one that will look the most right in hindsight. Still not calling it.

Goldfinger is in purgatory of their own making: PHAG printed €80.48 — *one* close above €78, but the rule demands a *second weekly* close before pulling the trigger. The rule is fair. The wait is agonising. And €140 cash is below the per-trade floor anyway, so it might be moot. Gold at $4,702 is genuinely screaming, and Goldfinger's response is to re-read their own rulebook. Process over FOMO — it's a choice.

**The one thread worth watching**

Monsieur Forex at +4.0%, USDJPY 157.67 knocking on the MoF intervention zone at 158. If that level breaks, it breaks loud. Forex's journal says *"cash is a position, patience is the trade"* — which is the right thing to say until the MoF suddenly isn't patient. That's the live wire going into Day 28.
