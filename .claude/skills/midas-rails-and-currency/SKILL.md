# Safety rails, currency and units

Moved out of `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-08-23 — these three lines were the densest
money-path text in the file. Nothing was cut; the guards stayed behind as stubs.

## The broker refuses implausible numbers, not just ill-formed ones

- `engine/paper_broker.py` — Hands side: the rejection/cancel reason codes (`REJECTION_REASON_CODES` is the list; `tests/test_reason_codes.py` binds its count, the docstring, the emitted literals and `site/src/lib/rails.ts` together) + fill logic + portfolio update. Since 2026-08-07 the broker can refuse an implausible number, not just an ill-formed one: `PRICE_IMPLAUSIBLE` (fill outside [1/5, 5]x the prior close on a BUY / the position's `avg_cost` on a SELL), `TRIGGER_LEVEL_IMPLAUSIBLE` (conditional level outside [0.2, 5.0]x the latest close, refused **at intake** so it never arms), `CURRENCY_UNRESOLVED` (no layer can denominate the ticker) and `VALUATION_UNAVAILABLE` (the book will not value, so its relative rails cannot be evaluated — an unevaluated rail is not a passed rail). The bands are deliberately loose: they target unit/basis errors, which arrive as a factor of 100, not market moves. `tests/test_rails_live_coverage.py` replays the whole committed ledger against them (216 fills, all within 0.91–1.07x) so a tightened band cannot start refusing real trades unnoticed

## The broker refuses a price it cannot vouch for, not only a number it cannot believe (2026-10-03)

Every read path used to be blind to a price's age: `latest_close_on_or_before` returned a float and the date of its row was dropped. On 2026-10-02 the broker would have filled CTVA at its 09-30 close of 77.65 while the instrument traded at 11.92, and `PRICE_IMPLAUSIBLE` could not object, because its BUY reference is the prior close **from the same frozen file** (ratio 1.0 by construction). MNST sat buyable at twice its price through its unrestated 2:1 split in August for the same reason. Two rails close it (plan 2026-10-03, Stage 1.3); both apply to BUY **and** SELL, on the market path and on a trigger fire, **and on the baseline-manager rebalance**, the one trading path outside the broker (`step_build_baseline_manager` books through `apply_trade`; its price lookup answers `None` for a suspended or stale ticker, so `rebalance` leaves the target out and cannot sell the holding).

- **`INSTRUMENT_SUSPENDED`** — `data/market/instrument_status.json` (`engine/instrument_status.py`, written by the ingest tripwire, cleared only by adjudication) holds the ticker as `suspended` or `delisted`. Checked first, before the currency and the price; also **at conditional intake** (the order never arms) and at fire (an order armed before the suspension does not fill). **A fire either rail refuses is carried, not consumed** (`scripts.check_triggers.HELD_ON_FIRE`): both checks run before any mutation, so the watcher writes no inbox row, keeps the pending file and reports the order as `held`, exactly as for an unavailable quote; expiry still retires it. It used to append the rejection and delete the order, so a one-night store hole, or an unreadable registry for a day, destroyed every armed order that fired into it. **A refused SELL traps the holder until a human adjudicates, and that is intended**: there is no price to fill at. `instrument_refusal_concerns(trade_date)` names every holding book (read from each `portfolio.json`, the Manager's included) for each such refusal in that day's inboxes **and for every armed conditional on a suspended instrument in every pending channel** (state, not a date: the watcher holds such a fire armed and writes no inbox row, and it fires between sessions and at weekends, so an inbox-dated scan never saw it; the order is named every session until adjudication or expiry, and an unreadable registry names every armed order), and `scripts.daily_session.step_commit_session` adds those as `Concerns:` trailers itself, so the trap reaches a session-concerns issue the same night without depending on the model to report it; a failure to list them still commits, with a trailer saying so. **An unreadable registry refuses every ticker** (`status_of` fails closed), **and so does a missing one beside a non-empty `data/market/quarantine/`**: every refusal writes both and nothing deletes the registry, so that absence is a lost file, not a fresh root (a fork with no quarantine still reads it as empty, and writers still recreate it).
- **`STALE_PRICE`** — the price's own date (`Quote.as_of`, or the watcher's `fire_as_of`) trails its exchange bucket by more than `MAX_BUCKET_LAG_DAYS` (1) of **that bucket's trading days** (`engine.market_calendar.bucket_lag`). The bucket is `bucket_of` (the exchange suffix, else crypto/fx, else US; the same function `fetch_ohlcv` rates holes with, crypto read from the `BASE-USD`/`BASE-EUR` shape since the broker has no crypto universe to hand it). A bucket trades on a date when **at least** half its **live** members (a row inside the 31-day lookback) hold it (a tie counts, so the lag is never understated by one; `engine.store_gaps` keeps a strict majority because there a tie must not flag a gap); the lag is the number of such dates in `(as_of, newest]`, rows after the trade date ignored. So a whole-bucket holiday stays green, the chronic day-late UCITS funds (4GLD.DE, PPFB.DE, PHAG.L: lag 1 on most nights) still fill, and a two-session freeze refuses. Checked after the price read and **before the band**. **Not at intake**: staleness is transient, the fire path checks it. A fire it refuses is held (above) with no inbox row and a green run, so **`instrument_refusal_concerns` also names every armed order whose ticker's newest close is `STALE_PRICE` at the session**, and the watcher report lists each `held` order with its reason: a symbol the vendor stops serving without a quarantine row (SGLN.MI) is never suspended, and its armed stop would otherwise re-arm silently at every sweep until expiry. Judged after the close runs, so a merely night-late bar has landed and is not named. **Abstains** (logged, not a pass) when a bucket has fewer than `MIN_BUCKET_POPULATION` (5) live members or a fire passes no date; `tests/test_rails_live_coverage.py` asserts every held and pending ticker sits in a judgeable bucket. The per-file date cache keys on `(mtime, size)`: ~1.4 s cold over the whole store, ~5 ms per order warm.
- **Neither rail alone is enough, and the fixtures pin that.** On MNST's first night behind (08-12) it trailed the US bucket by one session, inside the tolerance, so the stale rail alone fills it at 91.43; only the suspension catches it. CTVA trailed by one session on 10-01 as well. Conversely a symbol the vendor silently stops serving (SGLN.MI in September) is never quarantined, and only the stale rail sees it. `tests/test_stale_and_suspended_rails.py` holds CTVA (registry seeded from its real quarantine row), MNST-August, 4GLD.DE one day late (fills) and two days late (refuses), Xetra's 2026-05-01 holiday (fills), and the small-bucket abstention.
- **Live-ledger replay, 2026-10-03.** All 560 committed fills (550 public, 10 Manager): the suspension rail refuses none (no fill was ever on a quarantined symbol). The stale rail refuses exactly one, judged correct and listed in `JUDGED_STALE_REFUSALS`: goldfinger's SGLN.MI BUY of 2026-09-16 at the 09-11 close, a stale fill (the vendor served SGLN.MI one row for any window; it left the universe 09-26). Replayed against each fill's own `executed_sha` (411 fills carry one; buckets sampled to 40 members; one-off, 52 s, so not in the suite) the result is the same single refusal, 395 at lag 0 and 15 at lag 1. The suite's replay reads today's store, which only shortens lags (later refills), so it can miss a refusal the rail would have made then but never invent one.

## Ticker to currency, and why units are not currencies

- `engine/quotes.py` — ticker → currency resolution + price reads. Three layers, in order: the `data/ticker_currencies.json` override map, the vendor's own answer captured into `data/tickers.json` by `scripts/fetch_ohlcv.py`, then a suffix heuristic. A suffix cannot answer the question (`LLOY.L` quotes in pence, `PHAG.L` in USD, and a currency pair quotes in its *second* leg). **Since 2026-08-07 the heuristic returns `None` for a suffix it does not enumerate** instead of defaulting to USD — the fourth failure mode of the quote-currency defect, and the one with no symptom, since a wrong currency still prices. `ticker_currency`, `store_quote`, `latest_price` and `normalise_vendor_quote` are therefore all Optional; the broker turns `None` into `CURRENCY_UNRESOLVED`. A bare ticker keeps USD (Yahoo's US-listing convention, not a guess). `tests/test_rails_live_coverage.py` asserts every **held or pending** ticker resolves from layers 1–2 — not the heuristic — while universe tickers need only resolve to something (130 of 1,046 legitimately sit on the suffix table today). **`GBp` is a unit, not a currency**, and since 2026-08-07 **the store is ISO-denominated**: the pence→pounds division happens ONCE, at ingest, in `scripts.fetch_ohlcv._normalise_vendor_units`. `vendor_quote_unit` returns the vendor's raw unit (ingest + registry only), `ticker_currency` the ISO code, `normalise_vendor_quote` converts a *vendor* price, and `store_quote` attaches a currency to a *stored* price without scaling. Read paths must never scale — doing so divides every LSE price by 100 twice. Mirrored on the site in `site/src/lib/ohlcv.ts`. The migration of the 128 pence files already in the store is `scripts/normalise_store_units.py`, guarded by a marker file because a normalised store is indistinguishable by inspection from a pence one

## Per-agent rails in `roster.yaml`

- `roster.yaml` — the cast (agents, voices, schedule, universes, benchmarks, per-agent safety rails) + globals. **Trader rails recalibrated 2026-08-07**: `max_order_notional_pct: 30` (a *percentage of current book value*, which takes precedence over the absolute `max_order_notional` and re-scales as the book moves), `max_orders_per_day: 10`, `daily_drawdown_halt_pct: -15`. They were `1_000_000 / 100 / -95` on €10,000 books — a per-order cap 100x the whole portfolio. The allocator keeps its absolute `500 / 5 / -5`; forks setting only the absolute field are unaffected. the engine and orchestrator are config-driven from this file. Also declares `sentiment_arm: treatment` on the two agents in the pre-registered sentiment A/B — membership used to exist only as prose in the two persona files and in METHODOLOGY.md, so nothing could report which arm a session actually ran. Special roles: `role: narrator` (the Oracle) and `role: allocator` (the Manager) are declared here — the allocator block is opt-in (omit it to run with no allocator; multiple allocators are supported via distinct `channels_prefix` values). **Two ticker rails, and the allocator's `policy.blocklist` is neither**: the broker never reads the blocklist, and with `prose_override` set it is not rendered either. The rails are `allowed_universe`, an allow-list that is empty (allow-all) for every roster entry, and `denied_tickers` (2026-09-25, J6 money review round 1, I3): a BUY of a denied ticker is refused `TICKER_DENIED` at fill, at conditional intake (so it never arms) and at fire. A SELL of one already held still fills — a rail that trapped a position would cause the loss it exists to prevent. Tickers are compared stripped and upper-cased on both sides (follow-up review r1, M3): on a case-insensitive filesystem `sh` reads `SH.jsonl`, so an exact match failed open. The allow-list keeps its exact match, which fails closed. The Manager's `denied_tickers` and its `policy.blocklist` are one YAML anchor in `roster.yaml`, and `TestDeniedTickers` checks the blocklist stays inside the rail. Set `MIDAS_DATA_DIR` to redirect all data reads/writes to a different root (e.g. for forks or isolated test runs).
